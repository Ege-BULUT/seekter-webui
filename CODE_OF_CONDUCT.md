# Code of Conduct

## The short version

This project exists because job hunting is demoralising and the clerical half of it can be automated. People who show up here are often in the middle of that, and some of them are having a bad month. Be useful, be direct, and assume the other person is doing their best with what they know.

## What is expected

- **Be direct without being unkind.** "This note contradicts the one in `greenhouse.md`, and that file is the one a run reads first" is good feedback. "This is sloppy" is not.
- **Disagree about the measurement, not the person.** If you think a note is wrong, say what you ran and what you got.
- **Respect privacy, including your own.** Never post a real name, email address, phone number, home address or CV in an issue, a pull request or a discussion — not yours, not a recruiter's, not an interviewer's. Redact screenshots before attaching them.
- **Don't name and shame employers.** A note that says "this ATS discards the uploaded CV silently" is a contribution. A thread about how a specific company treated you is not what this repository is for, and it can follow a real person around.
- **Accept that some things are settled.** The guardrails in `CONTRIBUTING.md` — no CAPTCHA solving, no account creation, no passwords, no sending messages as the user, no invented answers — are not open questions.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory language or conduct of any kind, including about nationality, immigration or visa status, which comes up constantly in this domain and is never a joke here.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their explicit permission.
- Deliberate disruption: spam, off-topic recruiting, or pull requests that quietly weaken the guardrails or the leak scan.

## Scope

This applies in every space this project uses — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any place someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

If you see or experience something covered above, report it privately:

- Open a **[private security advisory](https://github.com/selfishprimate/seekter/security/advisories/new)** on this repository. It is the only reporting channel here that is not public, so it is the right place for a conduct report too, even though the form is written for vulnerabilities.
- If the report concerns the maintainer, or you would rather not use GitHub at all, use [GitHub's own abuse reporting](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse).

Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential. You will not be asked to justify why something bothered you.

## Consequences

Depending on what happened, the response may be a private word, a public correction, edited or deleted content, or a block from the repository. A single serious incident is enough for the last one. The maintainer decides, and will say what was decided and why.
