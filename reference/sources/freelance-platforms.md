# Freelance platforms and talent networks

Upwork, Toptal, Malt, A.Team and the rest are **profile channels, not job-board sources**: they are not part of the step 5 sweep.
- Upwork: not worth focusing on as a primary channel. Rates sit well below employment equivalents (one discipline measured at $20–50/h); average proposal reply rate is 7.45%; the client base is shrinking. Automation works for text and simple selects, but **not** for the hourly rate, date dropdowns or portfolio publishing; hand those to the user.
- Toptal: a talent account rejected at first screening shows only a paid "TopAccess" upsell ($29.95/mo × 12, "does not guarantee acceptance"). **Don't buy.** Reapplying needs a new account (password).
- Malt: day-rate model; 5% commission (10% for the first 6 months in FR/ES/BE). Check its country list against the candidate's.
- A.Team: no builder commission, under 2% acceptance; product and engineering roles only.
- Braintrust: free, US/LATAM-heavy. Third.
- Contra: pivoted to creator/AI work. Secondary.
- **Proxify** (Stockholm): a vetted talent network that matches contractors to clients on long-term engagements, not a board. Every "posting" it emits is a funnel into the pool, which is why it sits in the aggregator regex in `/seekter-run` §2.7 and why a Proxify link in a sweep is a skip. As a **profile channel** it is a real option, with caveats measured 29 Sept from their own pages:
  - They do place designers. `proxify.io/solutions/capability/design` lists Figma, UI, UX, Framer, Journey Mapping and Maze, and claims an average matching time of 2 days.
  - **The positioning is engineering-first and the design track may not exist separately.** `/hire-designers` 404s, the home page says "Vetted developers", and the design capability page's own description of the funnel is entirely a coding funnel: the assessment is "real-world coding challenges and bug fixing" and the next step is "live coding exercises with our senior engineers". No design-specific stage is described anywhere on the design page. Treat a designer's path through it as unproven.
  - They screen on **rates and location** at the ATS stage, before a human sees anything, and claim roughly 1% acceptance from 20,000+ monthly applicants.
  - Entry point `talent.proxify.io/apply` opens without an account and starts with "Upload CV" or "Fill it in manually". It goes multi-step from there and ends in account creation, so Seekter can start it and the candidate finishes it.
  - **Step 1 is fillable; the wall is at the end of it, not at the start.** Measured 29 Sept on the manual path, `/apply/personal-details`, "Step 1 of 4". It is an accordion of four sections, each of which must be closed with its own **Continue**/**Done** button before it turns Complete: personal details, location, phone (marked Optional but it has its own Done), then a single required tickbox for data processing. Notes on the individual sections:
    - Location is a "Search city" autocomplete. Type the city, click the suggestion, press Escape; the free-typed text alone does not count.
    - A tickbox next to it reads "I'm moving to a different timezone in the next 6 months." That is a claim about the future, not a location field. Leave it unticked unless the candidate has said it is true.
    - The phone widget has a separate country-code combobox that defaults from the location, so type the **local** digits only. It stores and redisplays the number in E.164.
    - Only when all four are Complete does **Next** stop being greyed out.
  - **If the email has ever been used, Next opens a login modal instead of step 2**: "There's already an account linked to this email. Log in to pick up where you left off.", with one button, "Log in to continue". There is no guest path around it and no password field before that point, so the whole of step 1 can be filled before anything stops the run. Steps 2 to 4 have not been seen.
  - **The account survives the rejection, and its profile stays fully editable.** Measured 29 Sept, after the lock above. `talent.proxify.io/my-profile` holds summary, skills with years and proficiency, work experience, education, portfolio and certificates, and all of it can still be edited during the cooldown, which is the useful window: the reapply date arrives with the profile already correct. Mechanics worth knowing:
    - **Uploading a CV auto-generates the skill list with invented year figures**, the same trap as Dice. Expect a long tail of tools carrying the candidate's total career length, including ones that did not exist that long ago. Tool-years is a never-guess item, so correct only the skills the profile actually states a number for and leave the rest for the candidate.
    - **Skills are not editable where they are displayed.** The rows on the profile page have no edit control and clicking them does nothing. The **Add skill** button opens the whole list as an editor: every existing skill with a stepper for years, a proficiency dropdown and the companies it is linked to. Change years there with the `-`/`+` steppers and Save once for the lot.
    - **Experience entries require company size and industry**, both as pickers, both facts about the employer rather than the candidate. Read the size band off the employer's LinkedIn About line (`51-200 employees`) rather than estimating it. The industry list is a fixed product-category taxonomy with **no software house, IT services or consulting entry**, so a services company has to be filed under what it builds; say in the tracker which bucket was chosen and why.
    - **Selecting an option does not close these dropdowns, and a stray click or keystroke afterwards reassigns the value.** Seen once: a company size set to `2-10` read back `11-50` after typing into what was meant to be the next field. Zoom in on the field after every pick.
    - Linking an experience entry to a skill is automatic in one direction only: saving a role adds its company to each of that role's skills, but the skill's own years are untouched.
    - **The skills editor refuses to save while any row is incomplete, and says so only in the vaguest terms.** Opening it appends an empty row at the top; if it is not used, Save returns "Please fill in all required fields before saving" and nothing identifies which row. Scroll to the top, find the row outlined in red, and delete it with its own trash icon.
    - **Years are validated per skill against how old the tool actually is.** Setting Claude Code to 2 was rejected with "You can have maximum 1 year of experience for Claude Code". The message names the skill, so it is easy to fix, but it means a candidate's own count can be overruled by the platform's idea of the tool's age. Save, read the error, correct, save again.
    - **An existing experience entry is edited from a pencil that only appears on hover**, at the top right of the entry. To give a still-open role an end date, untick "I am currently working in this role" first: the End date fields stay disabled until it is clear.
    - **⚠️ The monthly rate cannot be edited at all.** It is displayed under the name on `/my-profile` and it is the number Proxify screens on before a human reads anything, but there is no field for it: the card's pencil opens "Edit personal information" (name, main role, birthday, gender) and `/settings` holds only email, password and contact details. A wrong rate therefore needs a support request, which is a message sent as the candidate, so Seekter reports it and stops.
    - The same `/settings` page can also report **"Password creation expired ... please contact our support team"**, so an account can exist, be logged into and be fully editable while having no password the candidate can set themselves.
  - **And behind that modal the answer may already be no.** Measured 29 Sept. Where an account has been through the funnel before and was not taken on, the dashboard reads "Your Proxify application was unsuccessful this time" with a countdown to a **reapply date roughly two months out**, set by the platform, plus "We'll reach out via email then with a reminder". The `/apply` entry form accepts a complete, fresh step 1 from such an account and says nothing about the lock. **So on Proxify the login is not a formality at the end of the funnel, it is the only thing that knows the account's state.** If the candidate has ever touched Proxify, have them log in and read the dashboard *before* the form is filled; re-filling `/apply` proves nothing and the fresh submission does not exist. A rejection here is a dated lock, not a permanent no: record the reapply date and treat the record as closed until then.
- Skip: Fiverr Pro, PeoplePerHour, Freelancer.com, YunoJuno/Worksome, and developer-only platforms (Lemon.io, Gun.io, Turing, Andela).
- Day rate from a monthly band = monthly ÷ 20 working days, minus commission; it is also a seniority signal.
- Account creation and T&C/code-of-conduct acceptance stay with the user.
